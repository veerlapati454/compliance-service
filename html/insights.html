SOC 2 vs. ISO 27001: Which Do You Need First?

Enterprise buyers ask for one, global buyers ask for the other — and most companies eventually need both. Here's how to sequence them so you're not paying for the same evidence twice.

  • US-based SaaS selling to enterprise? Start with SOC 2 Type I, then layer ISO.
  • Selling into the EU or APAC? ISO 27001's certificate travels further.
  • Roughly 70% of your evidence base is reusable across both frameworks.
