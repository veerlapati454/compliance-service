HOW ENGAGEMENTS RUN

From kickoff to certificate, in six stages

The same sequence runs for every framework — only the control set changes.

01

Discovery call

We scope your systems, data flows, and target framework in a 45-minute working session.

02

Gap analysis

Every required control is checked against what you already have, with a written remediation plan.

03

Remediation

Your advisor drafts policies and configures controls alongside your engineering and ops teams.

04

Evidence collection

Automated and manual evidence lands in the vault as it's produced — nothing scrambled at the end.

05

Audit support

We sit in on fieldwork with your chosen auditor and answer control questions in real time.

06

Continuous monitoring

Once certified, control drift is flagged automatically so the next renewal is a formality.
